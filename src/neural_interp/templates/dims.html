Correlation and kurtosis

Left: correlation between dimensions across the vocabulary, ordered by tree. Right: excess kurtosis of each dimension's values over the vocabulary, raw against rotated. A rotation mixes dimensions and pulls kurtosis toward the Gaussian, so raw dimensions far above the rotated ones are special in this basis.

Trees
