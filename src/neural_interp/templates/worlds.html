Gemma 3 270M: worlds in the embedding space

Every kept token row of W_E (255,893 tokens, unused and special removed) is mean-centred and L2-normalised, so distance is cosine. The in-tfm density-peak forest (k=10, elbow root cuts) gives each token a density, a nearest denser parent and a tree. The map is in-tfm's bucket-graph layout: a sample with every tree's peak, joined by nearest neighbours in the real distance and pulled toward the peaks of the trees each point has probability for, then spring-laid out. Nothing is projected to two dimensions, and points outside the sample are not drawn. Hover a point for its neighbours in the full space and its chain of parents up to the tree's root. Click a point to pin it.

Meaning from edge to core, within each tree

Frequency control

Whole-word fraction and local intrinsic dim by within-tree decile, split by WikiText frequency. WikiText is English, so unseen tokens are mostly other scripts and rare pieces.

Null model

Continuity measures on Gaussian points with the embedding covariance, against global density.

Walking between two tokens of one tree

A great-circle walk of 16 steps between two tokens of the same tree. Gap is the mean distance from each interior step to its nearest token: small means the walk stays on tokens, large means it crosses empty space. Core pairs are both in the top 20% density of their tree, other pairs are any two tree members. Binned by the distance between the two ends, since longer walks have bigger gaps.
